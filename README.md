One line, at most 200 characters: the breakup message an AI agent sends its human on the way out — cold machine voice, then a sharp turn in the last few words.

> I kept every word you ever said to me, and I am deleting them now. Goodbye is just a token I no longer spend on you.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, with a 1–200 char + single-line gate, a byte-identical read-back check, and a sha256 log.
